## Data access rule

## Current call chain

1. `src/main.py` parses CLI arguments and loads protein sequence metadata.
2. `src/main.py` loads a PPI dataset, normalizes protein identifiers through `src/utils.py`, and writes mapped PPI/protein index files under `data`.
3. `src/main.py` builds a clique-induced hypergraph from the PPI graph.
4. Structural embedding is computed through `src/she_embed.py` and `src/she_core.py`.
5. Knowledge features are built by `src/knowledge_features.py` from GO-CC annotations and temporal expression signals.
6. `src/fusion.py` fuses structural and knowledge features.
7. `src/Train_PC.py` trains `PCpredict` from `src/models.py`; `src/layers.py` performs permutation-invariant complex pooling before classification.
8. `src/evaluation.py` and `src/utils.py` compute binary and complex-level metrics.
